            Learned on 2026-08-03.             What's required vs asked

            Optional fields still appear in the wizard — they're just skippable
            SectionRequiredAlso asked, optional
            1 · Where you live Country City State abroad
            2 · Roots in AP Constituency Mandal Village · district is derived, never asked

            Five required fields, not thirteen. Country, city, constituency, mandal and gender unlock the dashboard. District is derived from constituency and never asked. Everything else sits in the same wizard with an optional label, so nobody has to hunt for it later.
            Typing, not dropdowns. Country, city, constituency, mandal and village are all search-as-you-type against a fixed list — you type freely, but what gets stored is always a known value. That's what makes five requirements reasonable where thirteen typed fields weren't, and how Düsseldorf and Duesseldorf stop being two cities.
            The gate is after the account exists, not before. That's the whole change. A verified member who has already committed can be asked for more — a stranger looking at a signup form cannot. Same fields, far better completion.
